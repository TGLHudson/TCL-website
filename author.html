About

Tim Hudson works on operational risk, safety culture and barrier analysis, through Hudson Global Consulting in Cambridge, United Kingdom, and Leiden, The Netherlands. His consulting and methods practice is Hudson Risk Culture. He has published some 25 peer-reviewed conference papers and book chapters with the Society of Petroleum Engineers.

His position is simple to state. The work is dangerous by nature, and worth doing. Safety is the condition of being able to do it again, and the people who do it are the ones with most at stake. The work is pro-worker and pro-union.

The ladder and its origin

The safety culture ladder originates with Professor P. T. W. Hudson, the author's father, who developed the five stages with Professor James Reason, building on Ron Westrum's three cultures. The Culture Ladder is the son's account of why his father's ladder works, and where it should be read differently.

That relationship is stated here because readers deserve to know it, and because the book makes the same declaration.
