Request a copy of a paper

Most of our papers are published by the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE) and sit behind the paywall of OnePetro, its online library. If you cannot reach one through a library or employer, we will send you a copy for your own study.

Tell us the paper's number (for example SPE-220464-MS) or its title, and what you are working on if you would like to. The full list is on the Papers page.

Request route: an email address or form on this domain. Once chosen, the Request a copy links on the Papers page can open an email with the paper number already in the subject line.
