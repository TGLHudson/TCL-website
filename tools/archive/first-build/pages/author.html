About

Tim Hudson works on operational risk, safety culture and barrier analysis, through Hudson Global Consulting in Leiden. He has published some 25 peer-reviewed conference papers and book chapters with the Society of Petroleum Engineers.

His position is simple to state. The work is dangerous by nature, and worth doing. Safety is the condition of being able to do it again, and the people who do it are the ones with most at stake. The work is pro-worker and pro-union.

A paragraph in the author's own voice: how he came to the work, and what he has seen that the book draws on.

The ladder and its origin

The safety culture ladder originates with Dr P. T. W. Hudson, the author's father, who developed the five stages from Ron Westrum's three cultures. The Culture Ladder is the son's account of why his father's ladder works, and where it should be read differently.

That relationship is stated here because readers deserve to know it, and because the book makes the same declaration.
