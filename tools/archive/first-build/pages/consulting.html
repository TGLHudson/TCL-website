Consulting

Fit the culture to the risk

The book sets out the method. The consulting applies it to your operation: where your culture stands, where your risk sets the floor, and what to build, keep or stop.

Everything in this section is a proposed outline drawn from the book and the author's stated areas of work. Replace with the services Hudson Global Consulting actually offers, in the author's words.

Culture fit assessment

A structured reading of where each unit stands on the ladder, taken from the decision structures in place and what people are seen to do, set against the floor its risk space demands. The output is a footprint: where the culture fits, where it falls short, and where effort is buying cover against risk the work does not run.

Transition support

Help building the structures of the next rung, and checking that they are in place, in operation, effective and sustainable, rather than declared.

Leadership workshops and keynotes

Sessions for senior leaders and safety professionals on reading the ladder as fit, and on why most attempts to change culture produce motion rather than progress.

Board briefings

The questions a board should ask of its operation, and how to tell a genuine answer from a performative one.

Barrier analysis

Barrier and bowtie analysis of major hazards, with attention to which barriers depend on judgement and who holds it.

Operational risk review

Independent review of how risk decisions are actually made on a specific operation or project.
