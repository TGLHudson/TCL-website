The argument

A floor to hold, not a summit to reach

A safe organisation is one whose culture fits the risk it actually runs. How high it has climbed is the wrong measure.

The safety culture ladder names five cultures, from Pathological to Generative. Read as a scoreboard, it tells every organisation to climb. Read as this book reads it, it tells each organisation where the risk it runs sets the floor, and how wide a margin above that floor the moving ground and the uncertain reading justify.

A village bakery does not need a Generative culture. A deepwater rig cannot survive a Reactive one. Fit points both ways.

More about the book

Four questions

What the book equips you to answer

  1. Risk space. What does the work actually demand of us?
  2. Structure. Where are risk decisions made, and who holds the authority to make them?
  3. Skill. Who has the competence and experience to carry that judgement?
  4. Footprint. Where does our culture match our risk, and where is the gap dangerous?
