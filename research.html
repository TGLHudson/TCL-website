The research behind the book

The book draws together work presented over more than a decade, most of it at the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE) conferences on health, safety, environment and sustainability. Each paper sets out one part of the argument in fuller technical form.

Co-authors include Dr P. T. W. Hudson, who originated the safety culture ladder with James Reason, and P. A. R. Hudson. SPE papers are held in OnePetro, the SPE's online library; every entry below links to its Digital Object Identifier (DOI).

Every other source the book cites with a DOI is on the References page.

Request a copy. SPE papers sit behind OnePetro's paywall. If you cannot reach one of our papers through a library or employer, ask and we will send you a copy for your own study. Use the Request a copy link beside the paper, or the contact page.

Where the ladder comes from

The five-level ladder grew out of Ron Westrum's three cultures and the Hearts and Minds research programme. These are the papers that set it out and first tested it.

  1. A typology of organisational cultures Westrum, R. (2004). Quality and Safety in Health Care, 13 (Suppl 2), ii22–ii27. doi.org/10.1136/qshc.2003.009522
  2. Tripod Delta: Proactive approach to enhanced safety Hudson, P. T. W., Reason, J. T., Wagenaar, W. A., Bentley, P. D., Primrose, M. & Visser, J. P. (1994). Journal of Petroleum Technology, 46 (1), 58–62. doi.org/10.2118/27846-PA Request a copy
  3. A framework for understanding the development of organisational safety culture Parker, D., Lawrie, M. & Hudson, P. T. W. (2006). Safety Science, 44 (6), 551–562. doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2005.10.004 Request a copy
  4. Investigating employee perceptions of a framework of safety culture maturity Lawrie, M., Parker, D. & Hudson, P. T. W. (2006). Safety Science, 44 (3), 259–276. doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2005.10.003 Request a copy
  5. Implementing a safety culture in a major multi-national Hudson, P. T. W. (2007). Safety Science, 45 (6), 697–722. doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2007.04.005 Request a copy

Risk space: what the work demands
