References

Reference material

Every source cited in the book, in alphabetical order by first author, with a link wherever the source can be reached.

Sources with a Digital Object Identifier (DOI) link to it. Reports, laws, inquiry findings and papers without a DOI link to a free copy from the body that issued them, an official archive or an open-access publisher, where one exists. Commercial books and sources with no free copy are listed without a link. Papers from the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE) are held in OnePetro, the SPE's online library. The research programme behind the book is set out by theme on the Papers page.

Check that every link resolves before launch.
