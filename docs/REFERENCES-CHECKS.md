# References page: what to check before launch

The References page (`references.html`) lists all 326 sources cited in *The Culture Ladder* (manuscript v87). It was built on 2 November 2026 from the book's notes, with web searches to find missing DOIs and free copies. The search environment could not open doi.org or most websites directly, so **no link on the page has been click-tested**. Work through this list before the site goes live.

## 1. Test every link

- 126 DOI links. Most were printed in the book. About 40 were found by search, and SPE papers' DOIs were formed from the paper number (`10.2118/<number>-MS`); those are the likeliest to fail.
- 76 "Free copy" links to the issuing body, an official archive or an open-access publisher. These move more often than DOIs.

A quick way: open the page, and use a link checker browser extension or an online broken-link checker on the live site.
